Privacy Policy This app is used as a personal automation tool to upload video content to TikTok. The app may access basic TikTok profile information and upload permissions only after the user authorizes access. The app does not sell personal data, does not share data with third parties, and does not store TikTok passwords. Access tokens are used only to upload content to the authorized TikTok account. Users can revoke access from their TikTok account settings at any time.
